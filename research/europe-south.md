## 4. Application routes per country, and what a grade matcher cannot represent

### Spain

UNEDasiss (UNED) is the national body that accredits foreign secondary qualifications for Spanish public universities. (a) EU/EHEA systems, IB, European Schools, China, Colombia and the UK (listed in this group) obtain a UNEDasiss accreditation with an access grade converting home grades to the Spanish 10-point scale, optionally raised with PCE subject exams (at least 2 recommended) or subject recognition. (b) All other systems (e.g. US High School Diploma, Singapore A-Level, HK DSE) must meet requirements for homologation to the Spanish Bachillerato and, for public universities, take PCE exams (at least 4 subjects recommended); private universities set their own admission systems. Admission then runs through each region's pre-enrolment by cut-off mark out of 14. A predicted-grade matcher cannot represent: the grade conversion formula, PCE exam results, subject weightings per university, ministry homologation timing, regional pre-enrolment cut-offs, or the private universities' tests/interviews.

### Italy

Non-EU applicants resident abroad must submit a pre-enrolment (preiscrizione) on the MUR Universitaly portal; the university validates it (capped at the university's non-EU quota + 20%), then the student applies for a study visa (2026/27 visa deadline 30 Nov 2026). Foreign secondary qualification must follow at least 12 years of schooling; MUR Annex 1 (2026/27) sets national rules: UK GCE/IGCE with at least 3 A levels related to the course; US HSD with at least 3 APs (score 3-5) in different related subjects; IB Diploma with 24+ points (12 HL) and TOK/CAS/EE. ### France

Two national routes for first-year bachelor's: (1) Parcoursup - EU students and non-EU students applying to selective first-year tracks; (2) DAP (Demande d'Admission Préalable) - mandatory for non-European students entering first-year licence at a university, filed via the 'Etudes en France' platform for residents of 75 listed countries (incl. UK, US, Singapore, Hong Kong) or via the French Embassy otherwise; DAP window 1 Oct - 15 Dec 2026 for 2027-28, max 3 universities examined simultaneously, answers by 30 Apr 2027. Not representable by a predicted-grade matcher: route depends on nationality and country of residence (not qualification); DAP limits to 3 choices and normally implies French proficiency for French-taught licences; selective programmes and grandes écoles (Sciences Po, ESSEC, Polytechnique) run their own portals outside Parcoursup/Campus France with holistic scoring and interviews and publish no grade thresholds; public licences are overwhelmingly French-taught.
