Why you can trust the answers

Verified, or honestly unsure.

  • Every requirement is checked against the university's own admissions page. When we can't confirm something, we tell you — instead of guessing.
  • No made-up numbers. If a university doesn't publish something, Altiora won't pretend it does.
  • Deadlines move every year, so we point you to the official page instead of showing you a date that might be stale.
