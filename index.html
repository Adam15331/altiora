What Altiora does

Three tools. One clear path.

Built for students who want to understand their options before they commit.

Check Combination
Pick your subjects across 6 qualification systems and instantly see which courses are a strong match (green), possible (amber), or out of reach (red).
  • Works with A-Levels, IB, AP, Singapore & HK DSE
  • Filter by country, subject area, or grade
  • Grade thresholds shown per qualification
  • Admission test requirements flagged
Course Finder
Search by degree or university and see exactly which subjects you need, translated into your own qualification system.

Why we built this

Built by students, for students.

University admissions guidance is expensive, inconsistent, and often unavailable outside well-funded schools. Altiora exists to change that — giving every student access to clear, accurate subject-matching information, regardless of their school or background.

◎
Our mission
Equal access to university guidance

Subject requirements are scattered across hundreds of university web pages, written in different formats for different qualifications. We mapped them all into a single, consistent database — so you can search once and see your options clearly, whatever curriculum you're on.

◎
The data
295 courses · 6 qualification systems

Altiora shows subject eligibility only. Grades, personal statements, interviews, and admission tests all matter equally — always verify requirements directly with each university before applying. We flag admission tests where known, but the official admissions page is the source of truth.
